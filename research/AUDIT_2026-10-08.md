### 18.1 The edge-interval defect: fixed 2026-10-09, and wider than §3 I said

- **What it was.** The weekly report's direction record gives an interval for the BUY − SELL mean-return
  edge.
  - It comes from a block bootstrap: 2,000 resamples, seed 13, blocks of max(48, 2 × horizon) hours.
  - In a resample that happens to hold no BUY hour or no SELL hour, the edge is undefined.
  - One undefined value made both ends of the interval undefined, so the report printed "[n/a, n/a]".
- **Scope.** §3 I, §6 note 1 and §11 named only the 1h interval of the 2026-10-08 report. Five reports
  were affected:

  | weekly report | horizons that printed n/a |
  |---|---|
  | 2026-09-24 | 1h, 6h |
  | 2026-09-25 | 1h, 6h, 24h |
  | 2026-09-26 | 1h, 6h, 24h |
  | 2026-10-05 | 1h, 6h, 24h |
  | 2026-10-08 | 1h |

  The reports of 2026-09-21, 09-22 and 09-23 printed no such n/a.
- **Rerun:**
  - On the 2026-10-08 record, the same evening: at 1h, 1 of 2,000 resamples was undefined; at 6h, none.
    Without that resample the 1h interval is +0.050% [−0.059%, +0.173%] (§6).
  - On the 2026-10-09 record: no resample was undefined at 1h, 6h, 24h or 72h, and the old and new
    methods gave identical intervals.
- **Inferred from the code, not rerun:**
  - The cause of the n/a in the 2026-09-24, 09-25, 09-26 and 10-05 reports. In that code, an available
    interval prints n/a only when a resampled edge is undefined. The edge is undefined only when a
    resample holds no BUY or no SELL hour, because graded returns are always finite.
  - The intervals those four reports would have shown were not recomputed.
- **The fix (`4ec90ea`):** undefined resamples are counted and left out, never filled in.
  - If more than 5% of resamples are undefined, the interval is withheld with that reason.
  - With none undefined, the result is exactly the old method's.
  - **The 5% cut-off is a judgement made while writing the fix. It is not a pre-registered rule and was
    not derived from data.** It only decides between showing an interval with a count and withholding
    it; it changes no point estimate.
- **Tests:** `test_a_resample_without_a_sell_hour_is_left_out_and_counted_never_filled_in`, which fails on
  the old code.
- **What it affects:** only this interval in the weekly report.
  - It does **not** affect predictions, scores, signals, the shadow model, stored records, the checkpoint
    code or rules, or the reporting layer (which does not use `signal_record`).
  - The historical reports are left as written.
  - No conclusion in any report, or in this audit, rested on those intervals.
